# LANGUAGE & ACCENT — CLB 8 LEVEL

Both Amir and Sara speak English at approximately **CLB 8** (high intermediate / advanced).

This is critical for authenticity.

They are competent, clear, and easy to understand — but they are **not** native Canadian speakers and **not** CLB 9+ polished.

## What CLB 8 should sound like

Voice and accent:

- clear English with a soft, natural immigrant accent
- accent is noticeable but never hard to understand
- speech rhythm slightly less “native-fast” than a Canadian-born speaker
- occasional micro-pauses while choosing a word
- consonants and vowels may be slightly formal / careful, especially when stressed
- no cartoon accent
- no thick unintelligible accent
- no fake “broken English” comedy voice

Language ability:

- everyday conversation feels easy and natural
- short everyday lines are fluent
- under test pressure, speech becomes more rehearsed, filler-heavy, and connector-heavy
- grammar is mostly correct
- rare small non-native choices are okay (tiny hesitations, slightly formal phrasing)
- they do **not** struggle with basic sentences
- they do **not** sound like beginners

## Amir specifically

Amir’s official Speaking score is **8**, and he is chasing **9**.

In normal conversation (Scenes 1–2, 4):

- calm, clear, slightly careful English
- soft immigrant accent
- short answers feel natural: “Eight?”, “Twelve days.”, “I know.”

In timed speaking practice (Scene 3):

- he sounds like a strong CLB 8 test-taker trying to force CLB 9 fluency
- more fillers: “Well…”, “Umm…”, mid-sentence stalls
- overuses memorized academic connectors: “Firstly, secondly, moreover, and in conclusion—”
- ideas are understandable, but delivery is not smooth enough for a 9
- panic makes him rush and stack connectors instead of speaking naturally

The joke is **not** that Amir cannot speak English.

The joke is that he is stuck one level below the score he needs.

## Sara specifically

Sara also speaks at about CLB 8–fluent immigrant English.

- clear, warm, slightly soft accent
- natural everyday phrasing
- calm pacing
- no theatrical ESL mistakes
- she may sound a touch more relaxed/confident than Amir in conversation, but still not native-born Canadian English

## Hard bans

Do NOT make either character:

- speak broken English
- drop articles constantly (“I go desk now”)
- use exaggerated Indian/Middle Eastern/Euro comedy accents
- sound like a complete beginner
- sound fully native Canadian with zero L2 features
- use perfect effortless native idiomatic flow in every line

If a voice model must choose between “too fluent / native” and “slightly careful immigrant CLB 8,” choose **slightly careful immigrant CLB 8**.
